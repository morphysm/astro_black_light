# Decan sample — Saturn in Capricorn (draft v2, heavier, 6 Nov 2026)

Wired into the app on 6 Nov 2026 via `narrative.js` (Capricorn texts below are the approved sample).
Decan rulers: Chaldean order (Hellenistic "faces"). Two variants per text; the chart's own details pick one.
v2: balanced two-clause aphorisms removed (they read as I Ching); abstract nouns replaced by body, rot, cold and concrete.

## Decan I · 0°–10° · Jupiter's face — the institution

- Opening
  - A: Saturn in a ministry that has outgrown its dead. Wet plaster, typewriters still running in rooms nobody enters.
  - B: The building is enormous and it is rotting from the archive up. Mould climbs the files floor by floor.
- Weight
  - A: The law is carried in the body like lead shot. It settles in the joints and does not leave.
  - B: Every year a heavier ledger is chained to the wrist. The skin under the chain has gone grey.
- Structure
  - A: Corridors lengthen at night. The doors are numbered past any count, and behind one of them a clerk dies at the desk.
  - B: Stairwells without landings, lifts that stop between floors. The air is breathed again and again until it tastes of the first lungs.
- Endurance
  - A: The building stands long after its purpose has died inside it. Nobody has dared to remove the body.
  - B: The occupant dies at the post. The post is advertised the next morning, unchanged.

## Decan II · 10°–20° · Mars's face — the barracks

- Opening
  - A: Saturn in the barracks. Iron in the blood and frost on the iron; by spring both are rust.
  - B: The drill starts in the dark. Breath freezes, boots crack on the yard, someone falls and is left there.
- Weight
  - A: The fist is clenched so long the tendons shorten. The hand will not open again.
  - B: Violence is swallowed and kept in the gut. It ferments there.
- Structure
  - A: Bunks bolted to the wall, a bucket in the corner, a bulb that is never switched off. Order is kept by the sound of the next beating.
  - B: Concrete poured over the old trench. The dead are still in it, and the walls sweat in summer.
- Endurance
  - A: The body stands in the cold until it stops feeling the cold, then stops feeling.
  - B: The anger outlives the reason, then the face, then the name. Only the scar keeps the date.

## Decan III · 20°–30° · Sun's face — the monument

- Opening
  - A: Saturn at the solstice. A low white sun on a monument nobody looks at any more.
  - B: A portrait hangs in every office. The man in it has been dead for years and still watches the room.
- Weight
  - A: To be seen is to be stripped and measured under a hard lamp. The lamp never goes out.
  - B: Authority is a coat lined with lead. It is buttoned on in youth and buried with the wearer.
- Structure
  - A: The podium, the parade, the official photograph. The face is cast in bronze while the flesh behind it is still warm.
  - B: A staircase of rank lit from above, each step colder and brighter. At the top, an empty chair in an unheated room.
- Endurance
  - A: The statue outlives the face it was cast from. Pigeons foul the eyes. Nobody reads the name on the plinth.
  - B: After the longest night the sun returns, low and white, and shows the bodies that froze waiting for it.
