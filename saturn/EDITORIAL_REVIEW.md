## Saturn-return timing

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| User decision | Saturn Returns is the sole planned feature expansion. Tarot is out of scope. |
| Method | Return when transiting apparent geocentric tropical ecliptic longitude equals the chart's calculated natal Saturn longitude. Search windows are centered approximately 29.4 years apart; two-day samples locate possible zero crossings and bisection refines each contact. Retrograde re-crossings remain separate. |
| Coverage | First three return cycles after the natal or event-chart start. Event charts are described as event-chart Saturn returns, not as human life stages. |
| Time uncertainty | Calculate the midpoint target, then solve again at the minimum and maximum Saturn longitude found in the 97 sampled natal-time positions. A date range is shown when the same number of contacts can be paired across those targets; otherwise the midpoint date is labeled sensitive. |
| Interpretation | A reflective question about responsibilities and structures is shown as original editorial copy, not a prediction. Source review is pending. |
| Validation | Prototype calculation only. Independent ephemeris comparison of exact contacts, repeat passes, cycle windows and sensitivity ranges remains required before release. |

The approximate 29.4-year search center is supported by NASA's stated Saturn orbital period; it is only a search window estimate. [NASA Saturn facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/).

## General context additions — 5 November 2026

These short passages explain the subject and repeat intentionally across charts. They are not matched personal interpretations.

- Return meaning: brief summary of responsibility and discipline themes from [Astrodienst’s Saturn Return entry](https://www.astro.com/astrowiki/en/Saturn_Return). The indexed excerpt was accessible; the full page presented a browser challenge. The reflection questions are original editorial copy. The calculation method and pending validation status are unchanged.
- Roman Saturn: [Getty’s iconography record](https://www.getty.edu/cona/CONAIconographyRecord.aspx?iconid=901000195), covering sowing/seed and identification with Kronos.
- Kronos and later imagery of time: [Getty’s account of Hurtrelle’s sculpture](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/object/103RQE), covering the child-devouring myth and later association with Chronos. The app distinguishes that association from treating the deities as identical.
- Hindu traditions: [Walters Art Museum, object 25.39](https://art.thewalters.org/object/25.39/), identifying Shani among the navagrahas and explaining protective worship. The app makes no universal claim about karma or all Hindu traditions.
- Renaissance occult correspondences: [Agrippa’s Saturn passage, Book I](https://renaissanceastrology.com/agrippasaturn.html), covering lead, heavy materials and solitary creatures. The transcription has inconsistent chapter labels (XXVI above the text, xxv in its heading); the app cites the chapter title.

All summaries are brief original paraphrases with links. The history remains collapsed by default and accessible from every screen.
